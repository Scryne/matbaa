# Hazır cevaplar (Etsy mesajları)

Etsy'de mesaj API'si yok; mesajlar Etsy Seller uygulamasına gelir. Star Seller ve arama sıralaması için **ilk mesaja 24 saat içinde** cevap
gerekiyor. Aşağıdakileri Etsy'de **Messages → Saved replies**'a bir kez ekle, sonra tek dokunuşla gönder. `[köşeli]` yerleri doldur.

**Siparişim nerede?**
> Hi [name]! Thanks for your order. Each piece is printed to order by our US production partner, which takes 2–5 business days, then it
> ships with tracking. You'll find the tracking link in your Etsy purchase page as soon as it ships. I'll keep an eye on it for you!

**Beden**
> Hi [name]! The shirts are a unisex fit. If you like a relaxed or oversized look, go one size up. The size chart is in the last listing
> photo. Happy to help if you share height and usual size!

**Hasarlı / hatalı baskı** (Printify'a reprint talebi: Orders → Report a problem, foto ekle)
> I'm so sorry about that, [name]! Could you send a quick photo of the issue? I'll have a replacement printed and sent to you at no cost.

**İade / beden değişimi** (yanlış beden iadesi yok — politika)
> Thanks for reaching out, [name]. Because every item is printed just for you, we can't accept returns or size exchanges, but we always
> replace anything that arrives damaged or misprinted. Let me know if there's anything else I can do!

**Kişiselleştirme isteği** (v1'de yok; kayıt et — Faz 11 talebi)
> Thank you for asking, [name]! Custom names and dates aren't available on this design yet, but we're adding personalized versions soon.

**Teslim süresi / tatil yetişir mi?** (Printful/Printify ABD standart son sipariş günü ~11 Aralık)
> Hi [name]! Orders placed by [date] usually arrive before [holiday] within the US: 2–5 business days to print plus 3–5 days shipping.

**Toplu / grup siparişi**
> Love that, [name]! You can add multiple sizes and colors to your cart in one order. For 10+ shirts, message me and I'll check what we can do.
