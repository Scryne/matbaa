# Etsy mağaza vitrini

Alan sınırları (araştırma 2026-10-13, Etsy yardım sayfalarının arama özetleri): shop title ≤ 55 karakter; About hikâyesi ≤ 5.000;
announcement için Etsy sınır yayımlamıyor (kısa tutuldu, < 1.200); FAQ soru ≤ 300 / cevap ≤ 750 (ikincil kaynak). Kapak görseli
1600x400 (4:1), içerik ortadaki ~%56'da (mobil kırpma). Kişisel veri yok (ad, şehir, okul): vitrin herkese açık. AI ve üretim
ortağı beyanı her ilanın açıklamasında zorunlu ve var; About'ta da düz bir cümleyle geçer (Handbook: partner'ları listele).

Görseller: `varliklar/marka/etsy-shop-icon.png` (yüklendi), `varliklar/marka/etsy-kapak.png` (`scripts/marka_varliklari.py`).

## About — story
```
HobbyHeartPrints makes shirts for people who love a hobby a little too much. The grandpa who tells the same fish story every holiday. The knitter whose yarn stash needs its own room. The pickleball player who never stays out of the kitchen.

Every design starts with an inside joke that the people of that hobby really get, set in a retro, easy-to-read style that looks good from across the room. Designs are created digitally in-house with the help of design software and AI tools, and each one is reviewed before it goes into the shop.

Our production partner prints and ships every order on demand from the United States. Printing to order means less waste and no warehouse full of unsold shirts. If anything arrives damaged or misprinted, message us within 30 days with a photo and we will make it right with a replacement or a refund.

Thanks for stopping by, and happy gifting!
```

## FAQ
1. **How do the sizes run?**
   ```
   Our tees (Comfort Colors 1717) and our sweatshirts and hoodies (Gildan) are unisex and fit relaxed on most people. Size up for an oversized look. Every listing has a size chart with exact measurements in its photos.
   ```
2. **When will my order arrive?**
   ```
   Each item is printed to order within 2 to 5 business days and ships from the USA. Most US orders arrive in about 5 to 10 business days in total. You will get a tracking number as soon as your order ships.
   ```
3. **Can I return or exchange my item?**
   ```
   Because every item is printed just for you, we can't accept returns or exchanges for size or color changes. If your item arrives damaged, defective or misprinted, message us within 30 days with a photo and we will send a replacement or a refund.
   ```
4. **How should I wash it?**
   ```
   Machine wash cold and inside out, tumble dry low or hang dry, and don't iron directly on the print. This keeps the print bright for longer.
   ```
5. **Can I send it as a gift?**
   ```
   Yes. Enter the recipient's shipping address at checkout and the order ships straight to them.
   ```
6. **Do you ship outside the US?**
   ```
   Right now we ship within the United States only, and US shipping is free.
   ```
7. **Do you take custom orders?**
   ```
   Not at the moment, but new designs are added to the shop regularly. Favorite the shop to see them first.
   ```
